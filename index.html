THE PEOPLE'S OVAL OFFICE // ARCHIVE

‘America Deserves a President Who Cares About the 99%, Not Just the 1%’

By Luke Maloney Socialist Party Presidential Nominee · Verbatim Transcript

Thank you everyone for letting me be the presidential nomination for the Socialist party. My name is Luke Maloney, I am 37 years old, and I am from Ventura, California. I am from a lower working class family, my moms side being Mexican and my dads side being White. I remember while growing up, my Mom barely had enough money to afford our one bedroom apartment despite her working three jobs at once. When I got old enough to work, I started off working at a movie theater in high school, through the years working there I realised that the older people working there that were living on their own now, were barely getting by because how low the pay was for how expensive everything was, also during this time it finally clicked in my head that basic human necessities that you need to live like, water food, and hygiene products were being charged for high money was dumb. It was around this time when I became a socialist.

After high school I went to college to major in political science as well as becoming a socialist activist as I still am today. During my younger days, I mainly wrote articles about global and political issues for my school, did a lot of organization work like helping the homeless, and even joined the Peace Corps for a little bit to help people overseas in underdeveloped countries. I was able to make a pretty good name for myself during this time and was also able to get a lot of people on board with my beliefs.

After that, I ran for mayor of Ventura, California and sadly lost though I did get a decent group of supporters from my campaign so when I ran the second time I won and became mayor. Me being mayor made a big name for myself and I was able to get supporters for people way out of my region of where I was representing.

My campaign is for the people, and for the people and for the people to be able to live, we pay to be high enough to get by in life, things like water, food, and hygiene products should be free, and Universal healthcare and you know why? because I care for the people, I am one of the people, and you should not be giving all of your life saving to some rich guy just so you can barely get by in life, because those “people” are not for the people, they are for themselves, and if you want to fight for the people, you need to vote for me, fight for basic necessities, fight so you don't have to be poor anymore because despite a lot people that it’s your fault that your poor, well look at the prices of everything and then look how much your job is even paying you. I want workers rights, I want to try to pull out of every war/conflict currently going on so less people will die.

We have had many many presidents from both the right and the “left” that claimed they would help the people and they did not. So I have this to say, if nothing goes right, go left, and remember to vote for me. America deserves a president who cares about the 99%, not just the 1%.

Luke Maloney · Socialist Party Nomination Address ■
